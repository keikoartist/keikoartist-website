Frequently asked questions

Is a PNGTuber cheaper than a Live2D VTuber?

Yes. A PNGTuber starts at $60; a Live2D model with rigging starts at $150.

Can a PNGTuber become a Live2D model later?

Yes. The same character design can be redrawn in layers and rigged for Live2D when you are ready.

Do I need a webcam?

For Live2D, a webcam or an iPhone is used for face tracking in VTube Studio. A PNGTuber only needs your microphone.

Which is better for a debut?

Most debuts use a Live2D model because it moves with you. A debut package bundles the model with emotes, a background and an overlay.
