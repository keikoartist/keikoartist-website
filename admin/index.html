🔔 Discord notifications

Get a Discord message for every new order and website chat, so you see them on your phone right away.

Checking…

  2. Choose the channel for the notifications, then tap Copy Webhook URL.
  3. Paste it below, tap Save, then Send test.

✉️ Emails to clients (from your Gmail)

Order confirmations and invoices are sent from your own Gmail, so they reach the client's inbox instead of spam.

Checking…
  1. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords with your Gmail (2-Step Verification must be on).
  2. Type a name, for example KeikoArts website, and tap Create.
  3. Copy the 16 letters, paste them below, tap Save, then Send test.
