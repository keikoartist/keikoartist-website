Frequently asked questions

How much does a custom VTuber model cost?

Art only costs $100 (bust), $150 (half body) or $250 (full body). Art + rig costs $150, $250 or $400. Complex designs with wings or many accessories may cost a bit more; the final price is on your invoice. See all prices.

How long does a VTuber model take?

About 25–40 days, depending on the complexity of the design and the queue. Rush delivery (+25%) is available in the order form.

Does the model work with VTube Studio?

Yes. Art + Rig and Rig only orders include a VTube Studio file, ready to stream with webcam or iPhone face tracking. Art only orders get a layered PSD that is ready to rig.

Can I use my model on monetized streams?

Yes. Streaming, monetized videos and social media are included. Merch and other commercial rights are an add-on (+$30).

I already have VTuber art. Can you just rig it?

Yes, rig only starts at $100. Send your layered PSD with the order form. See Live2D rigging.

How do I pay?

After you send the order form, I email you an invoice as a PDF. Pay via Ko-fi in USD: in full, 50/50 (half upfront, half before final files), or in 3 installments for orders above $150.
