Frequently asked questions

How much do custom Twitch emotes cost?

Static emotes are $5 each and animated emotes are $15 each. A sub badge set (3 badges) is +$15. 13% tax is added on the invoice.

What sizes and files do I get?

Transparent PNG files for static emotes and GIF files for animated emotes, in 112, 56 and 28 px. Twitch-ready resizing is included.

How long do emotes take?

About 5–7 days, depending on how many you order and the queue. Rush delivery (+25%) is available in the order form.

Can you draw emotes of my VTuber or original character?

Yes. Attach a reference of your character in the order form and list the expressions you want.

Can I use the emotes on Discord too?

Yes. The files are sized for Twitch and Discord.

Can I use them on monetized streams?

Yes. Personal use, streaming, monetized content and social media are allowed. Merch / commercial rights are a $30 add-on.
