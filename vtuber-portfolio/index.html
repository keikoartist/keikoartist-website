Frequently asked questions

Who made these artworks?

Team Keiko: Keiko (artist), Zhopio (illustrator) and Tomy (rigger). Every piece here is original work, drawn from scratch.

Can I order something in a similar style?

Yes. Mention the piece you like in the order form, and attach it as a reference if you want.

Will my commission be shown in the portfolio?

Finished work may be shared on the portfolio and socials, unless you ask for a private or delayed reveal.

How do I order?

Click "Start a project", choose a service, describe your idea and send the form. I email you your invoice (PDF), and you pay via Ko-fi.
