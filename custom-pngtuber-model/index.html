Frequently asked questions

How much does a custom PNGTuber cost?

Bust-up is $60 and Thigh-up / full body is $100. Extra expressions are +$20 each; props and accessories are included.

Which apps can I use it with?

You get transparent PNGs that are ready for PNGTuber Plus or Veadotube.

How long does it take?

About 7–10 days.

What is the difference between a PNGTuber and a VTuber model?

A PNGTuber swaps between images (idle, talking, blinking). A Live2D VTuber model is rigged, so it follows your head, eyes and mouth smoothly. See custom VTuber models.

Can I use it on monetized streams?

Yes. Personal use, streaming, monetized content and social media are allowed. Merch / commercial rights are a $30 add-on.
