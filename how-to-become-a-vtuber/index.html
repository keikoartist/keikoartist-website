How to Become a VTuber: What You Need to Start

A VTuber streams or makes videos as an animated character instead of on camera. You need four things: a character, a model, tracking software and a way to stream. Here is each step, with real prices.

✦ Cute meets gothic, made from scratch with updates at every stage · 200+ commissions completed

The 5 steps

  1. Create your character. Name, personality, colors and outfit. A few reference images or a short description is enough; I design the rest with you.
  2. Choose your model type. A PNGTuber (from $60) switches images when you talk. A Live2D model (art + rig from $150) follows your face. Compare them.
  3. Get the model made. Every commission is done within 3 weeks, with updates at every stage. Live2D models come with a VTube Studio file.
  4. Set up tracking. VTube Studio tracks your face with a webcam or an iPhone. A PNGTuber only needs your microphone (PNGTuber Plus or Veadotube).
  5. Start streaming. Add the model to OBS or Streamlabs and go live on Twitch, YouTube or TikTok. Emotes, a background and an overlay make your channel look complete.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to become a VTuber?

A character design, a model (PNGTuber or Live2D), tracking software such as VTube Studio, and streaming software such as OBS. A webcam or iPhone is used for Live2D face tracking.

How much does it cost to become a VTuber?

You can start with a PNGTuber from $60. A Live2D model with rigging starts at $150 for a bust. 13% tax is added.

Do I need to draw my own model?

No. Most VTubers commission an artist. Send your idea or references and the artist designs, draws and rigs the model.

How long does it take to get a VTuber model?

Every commission at KeikoArts is done within 3 weeks, with updates at every stage.

Can I use a VTuber model on YouTube and TikTok?

Yes. Streaming, monetized videos and social media are included. Merch / commercial rights are a $30 add-on.
