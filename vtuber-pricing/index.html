Payment and rules

How you pay

Right after you send the order form, with the PayPal button. Pay with a one-click PayPal link (card or PayPal balance) in USD: in full, 50/50 (half upfront, half before final files), or in 3 installments for orders above $150. 13% tax is added on every invoice.

Custom requests

Have an idea that isn't listed? With a custom inquiry you name your price; 13% tax is added on the invoice and it's paid upfront.

Rush and complex designs

Rush delivery is +25%. Very complex designs (for example wings or many accessories) may cost a bit more; the final price is always on your invoice before you pay.

Revisions

Unlimited small revisions during the sketch. Up to 2 minor revisions after coloring; major changes after approval may have extra fees.

Refunds

Full refund before work starts. After the sketch, refunds are based on completed work. No refund after final files are delivered.

Usage rights

Personal use, streaming, monetized content and socials are included. Merch / commercial rights are a $30 add-on for any service.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a custom VTuber model cost?

Art only starts at $100 (bust); art + rig at $150. A full-body model with rigging is $400. See custom VTuber models.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes: 50/50 (half upfront, half before final files), or 3 installments for orders above $150.

Which currency do you use?

All prices are in US dollars (USD), before 13% tax, which is added on the invoice. You pay with a one-click PayPal link (card or PayPal balance).

Why do some prices say "from"?

It is the starting price of the smallest package. The final price depends on the options and add-ons you choose; the order form shows your total before you pay.

What does a VTuber debut package include?

Starter · Bust up ($250): Bust-up model art + Live2D rig + 3 expressions; Debut · Half body ($380): Half-body model + rig + 3 expressions + 6 emotes + 1 background + overlay; Full Debut · Full body ($520): Full-body model + rig + 3 expressions + 6 emotes + background + overlay + PFP + VTuber logo + stream screens.
