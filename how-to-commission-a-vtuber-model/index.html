How to Commission a Custom VTuber Model

Commissioning your first VTuber model is easier than it looks. Here is the whole process at KeikoArts, from your first idea to going live in VTube Studio, with what to prepare at each step.

✦ Cute meets gothic, made from scratch with updates at every stage

Start your commissionSee the costs

Step by step

  1. Decide what you needArt only (a layered PSD), Art + Rig (a model ready to stream) or Rig only (for art you already have). Then pick bust, half body or full body.
  2. Prepare your characterA character sheet helps, but it is not required. A description, a few reference pictures and the mood you want are enough to start.
  3. Send the order formChoose the options and extras, describe your character and attach your references. The total updates as you choose.
  4. Get your invoiceI review your request and email an invoice (PDF), usually within 6 hours (WIB, GMT+7). Your slot is secured once payment is received.
  5. Sketch and revisionsSmall revisions are unlimited during the sketch, so the design can settle. Up to 2 minor revisions after coloring.
  6. Lineart, coloring and riggingProgress updates at every stage. For Art + Rig I set up head turns, blinking, lip-sync and hair and clothing physics.
  7. Go liveYou receive your files and a VTube Studio model. Every commission takes 3 weeks at most; rush delivery (+25%) is available.

What to send with your order

Helpful to include

  • A front view of your character (a sketch, sheet or picrew is fine)
  • Colors you want and colors to avoid
  • Personality and stream style
  • Outfit details and accessories

Good to know

  • Extra expressions and toggles are +$20 each
  • Extra outfits or hairstyles can be added later
  • Questions first? Ask on Discord

Payment and rights

Pay with a one-click PayPal link (card or PayPal balance) in USD: in full, 50/50 (half upfront, half before final files), or in 3 installments for orders above $150. 13% tax is added on every invoice.

Streaming, monetized videos and social media use are included. Merch and other commercial rights are +$30. Read the full Terms of Service.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a finished character design?

No. A description and a few references are enough. The design is worked out together during the sketch stage.

How long does a VTuber commission take?

VTuber models take about 2–3 weeks, and every commission takes 3 weeks at most. Rush delivery (+25%) is available.

When do I pay?

After you send the order form you get an invoice. You can pay in full, 50/50, or in 3 installments for orders above $150. Your slot is secured once payment is received.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Small revisions are unlimited during the sketch. After coloring, up to 2 minor revisions are included; major changes after approval may have extra fees.

What software do I need to stream?

VTube Studio. It tracks your face with a webcam or an iPhone. Art + Rig orders include a VTube Studio file, ready to stream.
