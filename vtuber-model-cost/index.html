How Much Does a Custom VTuber Model Cost?

At KeikoArts a custom Live2D VTuber model costs $150 to $400 with rigging, or $100 to $250 for the art alone. This page shows what changes the price, what the extras cost and how to pay.

What changes the price

Size

Bust shows your character to the chest, half body to the waist, full body from head to toe. More of the character means more parts to draw and rig.

Art, rig or both

Art only is the drawing split into layers. Rigging makes it move in VTube Studio. Art + Rig gives you a model that is ready to stream.

Design details

Complex designs (wings, many accessories) may cost a bit more.

Extras and add-ons

Model add-ons

  • Extra expression / toggle: +$20
  • Hand toggle: +$30
  • VBridger: +$200
  • Extra outfit: +$100
  • Extra hairstyle: +$100
  • Commercial / merch rights: +$30

Other costs to plan for

  • 13% PayPal fee on every invoice
  • Rush delivery: +25%
  • Merch / commercial rights: +$30 (streaming and monetized videos are already included)

Smaller budget?

PNGTuber

An idle and a talking image with blinking, no rigging. From $60, about 7–10 days. Read Live2D vs PNGTuber.

Chibi VTuber

A cute full-body chibi with 3 expressions, ready to rig. From $45, about 10–14 days.

How payment works

Pay with a one-click PayPal link (card or PayPal balance) in USD: in full, 50/50 (half upfront, half before final files), or in 3 installments for orders above $150. A 13% PayPal fee is added on every invoice. Nothing is charged until you pay.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a cheap VTuber model?

The lowest-cost option here is a PNGTuber from $60. For a Live2D model, a bust with art and rigging starts at $150.

How much does a full-body Live2D VTuber model cost?

$250 for the art only, or $400 with rigging and a VTube Studio file, plus 13% PayPal fee.

Is rigging included in the price?

Only in Art + Rig. Art only gives you a layered PSD that is ready to rig. Rig only for your own art starts at $100.

Can I pay in parts?

Yes: 50/50 (half upfront, half before final files), or 3 installments for orders above $150.

Are there hidden costs?

No. The order form and your invoice show the total: the package, any add-ons you choose, 13% PayPal fee, and optional rush (+25%) or commercial rights (+$30).
