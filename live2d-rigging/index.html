What to send for rig only

A layered PSD

Rigging needs your character art as a PSD with the parts on separate layers (for example eyes, mouth, hair and clothing pieces). Attach it in the order form, or paste a link to it.

Your wishes

Tell me which expressions or toggles you want, and anything special about how the model should move. I review everything before your invoice is sent.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Live2D rigging cost?

Rig only costs $100 for a bust, $150 for half body and $250 for full body. Extra expressions or toggles are +$20 each and VBridger is +$50.

What do I need to send?

A layered PSD of your character, with the parts on separate layers so they can move. Attach it in the order form or paste a link.

Can you rig art that someone else drew?

Yes. Rig only is made for art you already have; just send the layered PSD.

Which apps does the model work with?

Every rig comes with a VTube Studio file. VTube Studio supports webcam and iPhone face tracking.

What is VBridger?

VBridger is an extra setup for more expressive mouth tracking. You can add it to your order for +$50.

How long does rigging take?

VTuber models take about 25–40 days, depending on the model and the queue. I confirm the timeline when I review your files.
