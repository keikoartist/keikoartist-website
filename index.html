Hi, I'm Keiko ♡ Digital artist & Live2D studio making original VTuber models, chibi art, emotes and stream assets. Cute meets gothic, made from scratch with updates at every stage.

How long does a commission take?

Illustrations usually take 1–3 weeks; full VTuber models with rigging take around 4–8 weeks depending on complexity and queue.

How do I pay?

Pick your options in the order form and you get your invoice right away: pay it with PayPal in one click, or via Ko-fi. Larger projects can be split into installments (50% upfront, 50% before delivery).

Can I use the art commercially?

Yes — streaming, monetized videos and social media are included. Merch rights can be added on request.

How many revisions do I get?

Revisions are free during the sketch stage. Small tweaks after coloring are included; major changes may have a fee.

Submit a request form. I'll reply within 6 hours by email or Discord to confirm details and the final price. Your slot is secured once payment is received.

2. Payment

You get an invoice right after ordering. Pay it with PayPal, or via Ko-fi, in USD. Choose full payment, 50/50 (50% upfront, 50% before final files), or 3× installments for orders above $150. Custom orders: you name the price and pay the full invoice upfront; 13% tax is added on the invoice.

3. Process & timeline

Sketch → lineart → coloring → (rigging) → delivery. You get progress updates at every stage. Estimated time is shown in the form; rush delivery is available.

4. Revisions

Unlimited small revisions during sketch. Up to 2 minor revisions after coloring; major changes after approval may have extra fees.

5. Refunds

Full refund before work starts. After the sketch, refunds are based on completed work. No refund after final files are delivered.
