Questions & answers

Terms of Service

Hi, I'm Keiko ♡ Digital artist & Live2D studio making original VTuber models, chibi art, emotes and stream assets. Cute meets gothic, made from scratch with updates at every stage. 200+ commissions completed so far.

How long does a commission take?

VTuber models take 2–3 weeks (3 weeks at most), depending on complexity and the queue. Smaller pieces like PFPs, emotes and banners take about 3–7 days. The order form shows your ready-by date.

How do I pay?

Choose your options, see your total and send the order form, then pay right away with the PayPal button: card or PayPal balance, in USD. For a Custom Inquiry I confirm the price with you first and then email your invoice. You can also pay 50% now and 50% when your art is ready, or in 3 payments for orders above $150.

Can I use the art commercially?

Yes — streaming, monetized videos and social media are included. Merch / commercial rights are a $30 add-on for any service.

Who works on my model?

Team Keiko: the art is drawn by Keiko and our illustrator Zhopio, Tomy does the Live2D rigging, Helen keeps your project on schedule and Piko answers your messages. You can see the team in "Meet Team Keiko".

Do I have to credit you?

No, credit is not required. You can use your model and art without mentioning me.

How do I get my files?

By email, to the address you used in the order form, once the last payment is done. You also see "Delivered" on your tracking page.

What do I need to use my model?

For a Live2D model: VTube Studio on a PC or Mac, with a webcam or an iPhone for face tracking. A PNGTuber only needs a microphone (for example PNGTuber Plus or Veadotube). Then add it to OBS or Streamlabs and go live.

Will my model work in VTube Studio?

Yes. Art + Rig and Rig only orders include a VTube Studio file, ready to stream with webcam or iPhone tracking. Art only orders get a layered PSD that's ready to rig. Extra expressions and toggles are $20 each, hand toggles $30 each, and VBridger setup is $200.

What's the difference between bust, half body and full body?

Bust shows your character up to the chest, half body up to the waist, and full body from head to toe. Art starts at $100 (bust), $150 (half body) and $250 (full body). See VTuber prices for each size.

Can I order only the art, or only the rigging?

Yes. Order the art only (a layered PSD ready to rig), art + rig ($150 to $400), or rig only for art you already have. Rigging covers head turns, blinking, lip-sync and hair & clothing physics. About rigging

What do I need to start a custom VTuber model?

Just your idea: a description, a character sheet or a few references. I design and draw your character from scratch, split the art into clean layers and rig it in Live2D. Custom VTuber models

Do you also make PNGTubers and emotes?

Yes: custom PNGTuber models from $60 and custom Twitch emotes from $5, plus banners, logos and stream overlays. See all prices or the portfolio.

How many revisions do I get?

Revisions are free during the sketch stage. Small tweaks after coloring are included; major changes may have a fee.
