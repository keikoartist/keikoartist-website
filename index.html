Questions & answers

Terms of Service

Hi, I'm Keiko ♡ Digital artist & Live2D studio making original VTuber models, chibi art, emotes and stream assets. Cute meets gothic, made from scratch with updates at every stage. 200+ commissions completed so far.

How long does a commission take?

VTuber models take 2–3 weeks (3 weeks at most), depending on complexity and the queue. Smaller pieces like PFPs, emotes and banners take about 3–7 days. Each service shows its estimate in the order form.

How do I pay?

Send the order form. I review your order and email you your invoice (PDF) with a one-click PayPal link: card or PayPal balance, in USD. Larger projects can be split into installments (50% upfront, 50% before delivery).

Can I use the art commercially?

Yes — streaming, monetized videos and social media are included. Merch / commercial rights are a $30 add-on for any service.

Will my model work in VTube Studio?

Yes. Art + Rig and Rig only orders include a VTube Studio file, ready to stream with webcam or iPhone tracking. Art only orders get a layered PSD that's ready to rig. Extra expressions and toggles are $20 each, and VBridger setup is $50.

What's the difference between bust, half body and full body?

Bust shows your character up to the chest, half body up to the waist, and full body from head to toe. Art starts at $100 (bust), $150 (half body) and $250 (full body). See VTuber prices for each size.

Can I order only the art, or only the rigging?

Yes. Order the art only (a layered PSD ready to rig), art + rig ($150 to $400), or rig only for art you already have. Rigging covers head turns, blinking, lip-sync and hair & clothing physics. About rigging

What do I need to start a custom VTuber model?

Just your idea: a description, a character sheet or a few references. I design and draw your character from scratch, split the art into clean layers and rig it in Live2D. Custom VTuber models

Do you also make PNGTubers and emotes?

Yes: custom PNGTuber models from $60 and custom Twitch emotes from $5, plus banners, logos and stream overlays. See all prices or the portfolio.

How many revisions do I get?

Revisions are free during the sketch stage. Small tweaks after coloring are included; major changes may have a fee.
