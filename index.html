Custom VTuber models, made from scratch

Full details

Every model starts from your idea: a description, a character sheet or a few references. I design and draw your character, split the art into clean layers and rig it in Live2D, so it's ready to stream in VTube Studio.

Bust, half body or full body

Bust shows your character up to the chest, half body up to the waist, full body from head to toe. Art starts at $100 (bust), $150 (half body) and $250 (full body).

Live2D art and rigging

Order the art only (a layered PSD ready to rig), art + rig ($150 to $400), or rig only for art you already have. Rigging covers head turns, blinking, lip-sync and hair & clothing physics. About rigging

Expressions and tracking

Your model comes with a VTube Studio file, so you can track it with a webcam or an iPhone. Extra expressions and toggles are $20 each, and VBridger setup is $50.

Your character, your rights

Streaming, monetized videos and social media use are included. Merch / commercial rights are a $30 add-on. Unlimited small revisions during the sketch stage.

Hi, I'm Keiko ♡ Digital artist & Live2D studio making original VTuber models, chibi art, emotes and stream assets. Cute meets gothic, made from scratch with updates at every stage.

How long does a commission take?

VTuber models usually take 25–40 days, depending on complexity and the queue. Smaller pieces like PFPs, emotes and banners take about 3–7 days. Each service shows its estimate in the order form.

How do I pay?

Send the order form. I review your order and email you your invoice as a PDF, and you pay via Ko-fi in USD. Larger projects can be split into installments (50% upfront, 50% before delivery).

Can I use the art commercially?

Yes — streaming, monetized videos and social media are included. Merch / commercial rights are a $30 add-on for any service.

Will my model work in VTube Studio?

Yes. Art + Rig and Rig only orders include a VTube Studio file, ready to stream with webcam or iPhone tracking. Art only orders get a layered PSD that's ready to rig.

What's the difference between bust, half body and full body?

Bust shows your character up to the chest, half body up to the waist, and full body from head to toe. See VTuber prices for each size.

How many revisions do I get?

Revisions are free during the sketch stage. Small tweaks after coloring are included; major changes may have a fee.
