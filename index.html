About

Hi, I'm Keiko! I'm a digital artist and Live2D studio creating original VTuber models, chibi art, emotes, and stream assets for streamers and content creators worldwide. My style mixes cute and gothic — soft pinks, dark ribbons, and lots of personality.

Every commission is made from scratch, just for you, with updates at every stage.

FAQ

How long does a commission take?

Illustrations usually take 1–3 weeks; full VTuber models with rigging take around 4–8 weeks depending on complexity and queue.

How do I pay?

Payment via PayPal invoice. Larger projects can be split into installments (50% upfront, 50% before delivery).

Can I use the art commercially?

Yes — streaming, monetized videos and social media are included. Merch rights can be added on request.

How many revisions do I get?

Revisions are free during the sketch stage. Small tweaks after coloring are included; major changes may have a fee.

    Terms of Service

    1. Ordering

    Submit a request form. I'll reply within 1–2 days by email or Discord to confirm details and the final price. Your slot is secured once payment is received.

    2. Payment

    PayPal invoice in USD. Choose full payment, 50/50 (50% upfront, 50% before final files), or 3× installments for orders above $150.

    3. Process & timeline

    Sketch → lineart → coloring → (rigging) → delivery. You get progress updates at every stage. Estimated time is shown in the form; rush delivery is available.

    4. Revisions

    Unlimited small revisions during sketch. Up to 2 minor revisions after coloring; major changes after approval may have extra fees.

    5. Refunds

    Full refund before work starts. After the sketch, refunds are based on completed work. No refund after final files are delivered.

    6. Usage rights

    • Personal and streaming use, monetized content and socials are allowed.
    • Merch / commercial rights on request.
    • The artist keeps copyright. No reselling, no NFT, no AI training.

    7. Portfolio

    I may share finished work on my portfolio and socials unless you request a private or delayed reveal.

    8. Agreement

    By placing an order, you agree to these terms.
